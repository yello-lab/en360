Do I need a laptop or coding skills?

No. EN360 is phone-first from start to finish. If you can send a WhatsApp message and download an app, you can follow every session. Bring your phone and charger — that's your toolkit.

I'm not tech-savvy. Will I keep up?

Yes. Every step is shown on screen, and facilitators move around the room during the build lab. You won't be left watching a cursor move.

I already use ChatGPT. What will I actually learn?

You'll move from one-off prompts to repeatable systems: your own custom assistant, a prompt library for sales and content, and automations that respond to leads without you. The goal is leverage, not novelty.

Is this for new or established businesses?

Both, as long as you're serious. Established businesses bring real workflows; newer founders bring an offer and a phone. You'll adapt the systems to what you sell.

What does my ticket include?

All modules and the live build lab, the prompt pack, lunch and networking, and your take-home implementation bundle. Founder's and VIP seats add post-summit support and one-to-one time.

Can I transfer my ticket or get a refund?

Tickets are transferable up to 7 days before the event. Refunds are covered by the Build Guarantee: if you don't leave with a working AI workflow, we'll work with you one-to-one until you do, or refund your seat.

Who is behind EN360?

Yellolab Solutions, a West African technology company building AI-powered systems for African businesses. EN360 is our flagship summit for founders and operators who want to run leaner, faster and further.

What should I bring?

Your phone (charged), a charger, and one real business bottleneck you want to fix. We'll provide the workbook, Wi-Fi and lunch.
